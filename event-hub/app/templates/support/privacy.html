Privacy policy

How BroncosHub collects, uses, and shares information for Santa Clara University campus events. Last updated November 7, 2026.

Who we are

BroncosHub is a student-built campus events application for the Santa Clara University community. Anyone can browse published events without signing in. Students sign in with a verified @scu.edu Google account to register, host events, and manage tickets. Related rules are in our terms of service.

What we save

BroncosHub saves your Google account identifier, SCU email, name, account role, and optional year level. It also saves events and images you post, registrations, ticket and payment records, reports you submit, event notification delivery records, and temporary abuse-prevention counters.

We use this information to run your account, manage events and registrations, track payments, send event notices when email delivery is enabled, and review reported events. Session cookies keep you signed in and help protect forms. We do not use advertising cookies or sell personal information.

Google sign-in data

When you choose Continue with Google, we request only these OAuth scopes:

  • openid — authenticate your Google session
  • email — your email address and verification status
  • profile — basic profile fields such as display name

We store your verified SCU email, Google account subject identifier (sub), and display name. We use Google’s hosted-domain and email-verification signals so only verified @scu.edu accounts can create a BroncosHub session. We do not request access to Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar, or other Google content for student sign-in.

BroncosHub’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide and improve BroncosHub authentication and account features—not for advertising.

Who can see it

Published event details, images, and organizer names can be viewed without signing in. Signed-in SCU users can open an event’s contact page to see the organizer’s SCU email.

Your profile and registration pages belong to your account. Organizers see statistics for their own events, including registration counts and year-level summaries. Admins can review event reports and moderation history.

Services we use

Google verifies your identity when you sign in. Render hosts the website, and Supabase stores the app’s database and uploaded images. When email delivery is enabled, the configured email service receives your email address and the event update being sent.

For Gmail delivery, BroncosHub sends registration confirmations, cancellation notices, and event updates from its dedicated account, broncoshubscu@gmail.com. We store that account’s send-only authorization privately on the server. The app does not request access to read your Gmail inbox, and students do not need to grant email access to receive these messages.

When paid checkout is enabled, Stripe processes payments for organizers. BroncosHub keeps payment references and status information, but does not store your full card number or security code.

Retention and security

Account, event, registration, ticket, report, notification, and payment records are kept while BroncosHub is operating so students can access history and organizers can manage events. Operators may delete, anonymize, or archive data when the project ends or when needed for security. Production traffic uses HTTPS, restricted session cookies, and CSRF protection on state-changing forms.

Your choices

  • Browse events without signing in
  • Update your name and optional year level from your profile
  • Sign out to end your BroncosHub session
  • Revoke BroncosHub access in your Google Account permissions

Children’s privacy

BroncosHub is intended for the Santa Clara University community and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Questions or deletion requests

You can update your name and optional year level from your profile. For questions about your information or to request account deletion, email {{ support_email }} from your account’s SCU address.

These requests are reviewed manually. We’ll clarify what can be removed and whether any payment or moderation records need to remain.
