Questions about an event

Open the event and choose Contact organizer. You’ll need to sign in with your SCU account to see their email.

Ask the organizer about entry requirements or refund eligibility before buying a ticket. If you already bought one, include the event name and ticket reference in your message.

Cancellations and refunds

You can cancel a free registration from My events. For a paid ticket, ask the organizer about a refund; they handle payments through Stripe.

If an organizer cancels an event or an admin removes it, registrations close and completed Stripe payments are queued for refunds. A refund is complete only when its status says Refunded. Check your registration for updates.

A pending refund may need the organizer’s attention. Contact BroncosHub support if you cannot reach them or the event was removed.
